Security 101
Understand the whole picture.

A guided introduction to how digital systems fail, how teams reduce risk, and what to do when a control fails. Learn the shared language of security across software, IT, and cloud without pretending to make you a specialist in a weekend.

BEGINNER · NO EXPERIENCE REQUIRED4–6 GUIDED HOURS12 SHORT LESSONS · 4 UNITS

Learn at your own pace. Most learners can complete it over 2–3 weeks at 3–4 hours per week, or in a few focused days.

Start Security 101 → Lessons and practice are free. An optional completion certificate may have a fee, shown before purchase.

By the end, you can

  • Explain confidentiality, integrity, availability, threats, vulnerabilities, and risk in plain language.
  • Trace a basic web request and describe what DNS, HTTP, and TLS contribute.
  • Distinguish authentication from authorization and identify where permissions must be enforced.
  • Recognize common input, session, data, and secrets risks in a small application.
  • Describe useful security logs and sensible first steps in an incident.
  • Complete a bounded security review with evidence, recommendations, and clear limitations.

A focused 12-lesson path

Each lesson includes a detailed explanation, a short knowledge check, and a guided activity you complete here. Expect about 20–25 minutes per lesson and 45 minutes for the final review.

01The security mindset3 LESSONS · 1 HOUR
  1. What are we trying to protect? 20 min
  2. Threats, weaknesses, and risk 20 min
  3. Trust boundaries and simple threat models 25 min

Build a practical vocabulary for assets, threats, controls, and risk. Practice with a fictional document service.

02Systems, networks, and identity3 LESSONS · 1 HOUR
  1. Computers, processes, and permissions 20 min
  2. How a web request reaches a service 25 min
  3. Identity, authentication, and authorization 20 min

Understand the basic systems security controls act on: processes, network requests, accounts, sessions, and permissions.

03Building safer software and services3 LESSONS · 1¼ HOURS
  1. Untrusted input and injection 25 min
  2. Sessions and access control in web apps 25 min
  3. Data protection, secrets, and encryption 25 min

Learn why common application failures happen and which design choices reduce risk.

04Operating securely and responding3 LESSONS · 1½ HOURS
  1. Secure defaults, updates, and dependencies 20 min
  2. Logs, alerts, and the first hour of an incident 25 min
  3. Final exercise: review a small service 45 min

Connect preventative controls to evidence and response in a bounded, beginner-level review.

What this course is—and isn’t

It is a breadth-first introduction. You will understand how the main security areas connect and gain a useful starting vocabulary for further study.

It is not specialist training. It does not qualify someone to perform penetration tests, administer production cloud environments, lead incident response, or claim a system is secure. Those are deeper courses with their own supervised practice.

Prerequisites: comfort using a computer and curiosity about how software or IT systems work. No command-line or security experience is expected.

How the learning space works

  • One lesson at a time, with a clear next step
  • Progress tracked as lessons are completed
  • Videos embedded in relevant lessons
  • Practice and knowledge checks stay in the course

Optional source material is clearly labeled; required activities do not send learners to another lab.
