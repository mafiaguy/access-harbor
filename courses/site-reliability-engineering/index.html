What you’ll learn

  • Define reliability from a user’s perspective.
  • Set service level indicators and objectives for a small service.
  • Use metrics, logs, and traces to investigate behavior.
  • Reduce manual operational work through safe automation.
  • Respond to incidents and write useful post-incident reviews.
  • Reason about capacity, performance, and operational risk.

Designed for

Software developers, operations engineers, and learners curious about dependable production systems.

Learning approach

Service scenarios, small calculations, public documentation, observability exercises, and an incident simulation.
