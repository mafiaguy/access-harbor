# Contributing to OpenTech Academy

Thanks for helping make practical security education more accessible. You can propose a correction, lesson, lab, or resource through a GitHub issue or pull request.

## Curriculum review criteria

- Teach a clear skill or outcome and put it in a sensible prerequisite sequence.
- Prefer free, public materials and hands-on practice with safe, authorized targets.
- Attribute sources with a direct URL, publisher or author, and access date. Link to third-party content instead of copying or embedding it unless the license and terms allow reuse.
- Check that instructions are technically accurate, accessible to the stated learner level, and still usable.
- Explain how a learner can tell when they have completed the lesson or exercise.
- Keep cybersecurity and cloud security content specific to their respective domains.

## Curriculum data

Use [`courses/COURSE_TEMPLATE.md`](courses/COURSE_TEMPLATE.md) when adding a course, and preserve stable, lowercase slugs. Each module should have clear outcomes, sensible prerequisites, lesson content, attributed free resources, safe practical work, and a completion checklist. Keep course pages as public static content; only describe the LMS as available when its real account and enrollment flow is implemented.

## Pull requests

Please describe the learner problem, the intended outcome, relevant sources, and any safety or licensing considerations. Keep changes focused and include screenshots for visible interface changes. Do not include credentials, private keys, or copied copyrighted material.

## Community standards

Be respectful, constructive, and welcoming to people at different experience levels. The [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) applies to project issues, pull requests, and discussion spaces.
