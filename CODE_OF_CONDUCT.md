# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want a welcoming, respectful community where people can learn and contribute regardless of experience, identity, background, or ability. Participants should act with empathy and make room for others to take part.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, patient, and constructive when giving or receiving feedback.
- Assume good intent while addressing the impact of words or actions.
- Share technical disagreement without personal attacks or harassment.
- Respect privacy, attribution, licensing, and the safety boundaries of security practice.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory or sexualized language, threats, doxxing, sustained disruption, and advocating unauthorized access or harm are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation in response to behavior that harms the community. Report concerns privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub. They will review reports fairly and keep them confidential as practical.

This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces when someone is representing the project.
